What is a HYROX Training Club

Training with structure. Not just hard.

A HYROX Training Club is an accredited gym that trains on the official HYROX programme. We don't make sessions up on the spot. They're built on the HYROX365 method, created by HYROX itself, and on coaches who race.

HYROX turns race principles into training for everyone. You don't need a start line in mind. You just need to want to get stronger, last longer and see yourself improve. And if you are aiming for a race, you'll be ready.

HYROX365 Group Training

Four focuses. One performance.

People aren't the same, so one class can't fit everyone. That's why HYROX365 uses four formats. Each builds a different part of performance. Together they get you ready for race day and for life outside the gym.

Base

Foundational

Circuit training with a purpose.

  • strength and endurance in one
  • aerobic capacity
  • technique, agility, mobility

Who it's for: ideal for beginners, still honest work for advanced athletes. Scales to every level.

Conditioning

Engine

Bigger engine. Longer output.

  • aerobic power and pacing
  • lactate threshold and tolerance
  • efficiency on the ergs

Who it's for: strong people who lack endurance, running or confidence on the SkiErg and rower.

Strength

Power

Strength that holds at kilometre 7.

  • max and repeated strength
  • strength endurance and power
  • moving load over time

Who it's for: runners and endurance athletes who run out of strength on the sled, lunges and farmers carry.

Race-specific

Complete

HYROX in training form.

  • running, stations and transitions
  • conditioning and strength together
  • race rhythm and mindset

Who it's for: anyone with a race date in the calendar who wants to know what's coming.

↻

You don't pick the focus. The programme rotates it for you. You show up and the coach knows what today is about. Over the weeks you go through base, conditioning, strength and race-specific work. That's how you build performance that lasts all 8 kilometres.

Race format

8× 1 km run. 8× station.

The order is the same at every race in the world. So you can prepare for it on purpose. And that's why every session here starts somewhere and leads somewhere.

Running decides

Half of your race time is running. If you can't run after a station, you lose time on every kilometre.

Transitions and pacing

The Roxzone, getting into a station, the first steps after the sled. That's where the minutes nobody sees go.

The head at the end

Wall balls after 7 km of running are a different exercise than fresh. We train work under fatigue, not just strength.
