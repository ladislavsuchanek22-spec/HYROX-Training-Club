01 · About the programme

Training with a plan, start to finish.

HYROX isn't a random workout. Performance is built on running, strength, station technique, transitions and tactics. Above all, on holding your output under fatigue.

Most people who get into HYROX train without structure. They go to classes, run now and then, lift now and then. Then they wonder why progress has stalled.

This programme fixes that. 12 weeks, a clear plan, individual zones set from your entry test. Programming that works for a beginner and an experienced racer alike. Plus coach support and a community that takes you further than you'd go alone.

How it works

You follow the plan on your own. Every Monday you get the whole week and do it in your own time and at your own pace. We recommend adding coached HYROX classes at Form Factory, where a coach helps you dial in station technique and work under fatigue. Classes are not included in the price.
